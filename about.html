About Vosik

Built by someone who knows what it takes to answer “Should we pay this?”

The supplier wants an answer. Operations needs the material. Finance wants the savings. Your purchasing team is expected to make it all happen.

Experience behind the product

The problem isn’t a shortage of things to check. It’s time to check them.

Vosik’s founder spent 15 years in purchasing, distribution, and supply chain — including a decade inside one of the nation’s largest homebuilders, after Sysco and Viskase. He built Vosik to address the recurring problems he and his teams faced: reviewing increases, comparing offers, following up on rebates, and explaining the results.

When that work lives across documents, spreadsheets, and inboxes, even an experienced team can struggle to keep up.

Vosik puts more of that checking power in your team’s hands.
