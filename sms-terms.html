1. About the program

The Vosik Appointment Communications SMS program is operated by Vosik AI LLC. People who opt in may receive non-marketing texts about appointment confirmations, scheduling changes, and follow-ups directly related to their appointment or request.

This program does not include cold outreach, promotional offers, or sales solicitations. Messages may be sent by our team or through our messaging platform. These terms apply to this SMS program; they do not replace a separate agreement for Vosik software or services.

2. Joining and giving consent

Age requirement (18+)

You must be at least 18 years old to participate in Vosik AI LLC's SMS program. By opting in, you confirm that you meet this requirement.

You join by providing your phone number and actively selecting the optional SMS consent checkbox on a Vosik form or booking page that describes this program. The checkbox is not preselected. Merely entering a phone number, requesting a quote, or booking a meeting does not enroll you.

By opting in, you authorize Vosik AI LLC to send the described messages to that number and agree to these SMS Terms & Conditions. You confirm that you are the subscriber or authorized user of the number and have permission to receive texts there.

You can submit a form, book an appointment, or purchase services without agreeing to texts. SMS consent is not a condition of purchase. If you do not opt in, we may communicate about your request through other appropriate channels, such as email.

3. Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies. The number of messages depends on your appointments, scheduling changes, requests, and replies.

Message and data rates may apply to texts you send and receive, according to your mobile plan. Vosik does not charge a separate fee to participate in this SMS program. Contact your wireless provider for information about your plan and charges.

4. Stopping messages

Reply STOP to any program message to unsubscribe. You may receive one final text confirming your opt-out. After that confirmation, we will not send further program messages unless you choose to opt in again.

We also honor other clear requests to stop texts. If you cannot reply or need assistance, email sales@vosik.io and identify the number you want removed. Opting out of SMS does not cancel your appointment or automatically unsubscribe you from email.

To rejoin later, repeat the original opt-in process and provide fresh consent. We do not treat a new appointment or an unrelated reply as permission to override an earlier SMS opt-out.

5. Getting help

Reply HELP to a message for support information. You can also email sales@vosik.io or call (865) 621-9493. The listed phone number is our business contact number; opt-out replies should go to the number that sent you the text.

6. Availability and delivery

Text delivery depends on your carrier, device, network availability, and other factors outside our control. Not every carrier or device supports every messaging feature. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

For time-sensitive appointment changes, contact us directly if you do not receive a response. If your phone number changes or is reassigned, let us know or opt out before giving up the number.

7. Your privacy

Our Privacy Policy explains the information collected through this program, how it is used, and your choices. Your mobile information and SMS consent are not sold or shared with other businesses for their marketing or promotional messaging. Providers may process information on our behalf as needed to deliver and support this program, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Do not send passwords, payment-card details, confidential purchasing documents, or other sensitive information by text. Contact our team for an appropriate way to share business materials.

8. Changes to the program or these terms

We may update these terms or change or discontinue the program. Updates will be posted here with a revised date. We will provide any notice required by applicable law and seek new consent before sending messages outside the scope of your existing consent. You may opt out at any time.
