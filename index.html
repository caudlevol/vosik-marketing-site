Protect the profit

Selling homes is hard enough. Don’t let avoidable material costs take more of the profit.

You can’t set mortgage rates. You can’t decide when buyers will move.

You can give your purchasing team a better way to examine the money going out.

A price cap nobody checked. A rebate deadline nobody followed up on. A supplier quote still carrying a cost that deserves another look.

Your team doesn’t need another reminder to save money. It needs help doing the work.

Where Vosik helps

The increase letter is only one place money can slip away.

01 / TARIFFS

“Why this much?”

When a supplier blames tariffs, Vosik pulls up the actual duty rates and market prices. Now your buyer can ask the right question instead of just paying more.

02 / AGREEMENTS

You negotiated it. Put it to work.

Vosik reads each supplier letter and checks it against the deal you signed — price caps, notice periods, the fine print. Then it drafts a reply your buyer can review and send.

03 / REBATES

Miss the deadline, lose the money.

Vosik tracks the rebates in your agreements and warns you before a deadline passes — so money you may be owed doesn’t slip away while your team is busy.

04 / MARKET CHANGES

Costs came down. Did your price?

Vosik watches the materials you actually buy. When market prices move, you get a short weekly note — so last month’s increase gets a second look when costs come down.

05 / BID COMPARISONS

The bids look different because they are.

Vosik lines up bids apples to apples — units, terms, rebates — so your team spends its time choosing, not building spreadsheets.

06 / REPORTING

Give finance numbers it can trust.

Reports that keep three things separate: money found, money asked for, and money confirmed back — plus what you paid versus what the contract says, per home closed.

07 / DIVISIONS

Same product. Different price in each division. Vosik finds it.

See what every division pays for the same material. When one pays more, your buyer gets a specific gap to take to the supplier.

08 / TURNKEY BIDS

Compare a package bid to itemized quotes — fairly.

One bid gives you a single total price. Another lists every item. Vosik breaks the package price into its real parts using published industry numbers — no made-up prices — so you can compare them side by side.

09 / VPOS

Double payments and missed back charges — found in files you already have.

Vosik checks your paid VPOs for payments made twice and back charges no one collected, then gives your buyer a specific item to go recover.
