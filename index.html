Where Vosik helps

The increase letter is only one place money can slip away.

01 / TARIFFS

“Why this much?”

“Due to tariffs” doesn’t answer the question. Bring relevant tariff information and market movements into the review before treating an increase as another cost to absorb.

02 / AGREEMENTS

You negotiated it. Put it to work.

Review supplier letters against price-change terms and flag apparent conflicts. Give your buyer a specific issue to take back to the supplier.

03 / REBATES

Miss the deadline. Miss the opportunity.

Identify potential rebates, track progress toward tiers, and get reminders to act. Keep the follow-up visible while other work competes for attention.

04 / MARKET CHANGES

Costs came down. Did your price?

Use relevant market changes to revisit quotes and agreements. Last month’s explanation deserves another look when the facts change.

05 / BID COMPARISONS

The bids look different because they are.

Compare units and rebate terms more clearly. Spend less time building the comparison and more time evaluating the decision.

06 / REPORTING

Give finance results it can examine.

Prepare purchasing reports that distinguish opportunities identified, actions taken, and results confirmed.

07 / DIVISIONS

Same product, different price by division — found automatically.

See what each division and market pays for the same materials, and give purchasing a specific gap to take to the supplier.
