That’s an extra $20,000. Whether you can challenge it depends on your contract and the situation.

What to look for

Three questions worth asking in any demo.

01

Does it find something useful?

02

Does it help us act?

Look at what your team gets back and how much work is left to do.

03

Is this work we do every week?

Pick a job your team repeats and compare the time against how you do it today.
