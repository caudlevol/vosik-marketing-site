Bring the facts together

The letter needs the context of the agreement.

Upload the relevant supplier letter and contract. Review the requested rate, effective date, and applicable price-change terms.

A specific issue to investigate

Thirty days’ notice. A sixty-day requirement.

Vosik helps flag an apparent conflict for your buyer to review. Check the applicable clause, exceptions, and supporting information before deciding how to respond.

Put the finding to work

Your buyer gets a response to review.

Vosik prepares a supplier email based on the findings. Your buyer checks it, edits it, and sends it. A draft is not an automatic rejection or a guaranteed saving.

What to look for

Three questions worth asking in any demo.

01

Does it find something useful?

Look for a specific issue your buyer would want to investigate.

02

Does it help us act?

Examine the output your team receives and the review still required.

03

Is this work we do repeatedly?

Choose a recurring job and compare the effort with your own baseline.

Make the next conversation specific

Bring a purchasing problem your team recognizes.

Tell Sales whether you want to evaluate supplier increases, tariffs, rebates, bid comparisons, or reporting. Agree on a suitable demonstration and any document-sharing arrangements first.
