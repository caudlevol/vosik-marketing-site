1. Who we are and what this covers

Vosik AI LLC ("Vosik," "we," "us," or "our") provides purchasing intelligence software for homebuilders. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information through vosik.io, our quote and appointment forms, and related email, telephone, and text-message communications.

The Vosik application at app.vosik.io and customer purchasing records processed within that application are governed by the applicable customer agreement and any accompanying data-processing terms. This website policy does not replace those agreements.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact and business details: your name, work email, phone number, company, job title or role, and other details you choose to provide.
  • Quote and appointment information: your selected meeting time, scheduling preferences, annual building-material spend range, purchasing needs, and any information you enter in a form or discuss with our team.
  • Communications: emails, texts, call details, messages, and related correspondence. When calls are recorded, recordings may be retained for service and quality purposes, subject to applicable notice and consent requirements.
  • SMS consent and preferences: the phone number you provide, your opt-in selection, the consent language presented, available consent timestamps and source records, and any opt-out or help requests.
  • Technical and usage information: IP address, browser and device information, pages visited, referring pages, approximate location, and other website activity collected through hosting, security, and analytics services.

We collect information directly from you and through the service providers that operate our website, forms, scheduling, and communications. Please do not submit confidential purchasing records, account credentials, payment-card information, or other sensitive information through public website forms or text messages.

3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to your questions, demo requests, and quote requests.
  • Arrange, confirm, change, or cancel appointments and provide related follow-up.
  • Understand your business requirements and prepare requested proposals.
  • Maintain contact and communication records and provide customer support.
  • Send appointment-related texts when you have separately opted in to that messaging program.
  • Operate, protect, troubleshoot, and improve our website and communications.
  • Honor your preferences, document consent, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations.

Providing a phone number or submitting a quote request does not, by itself, enroll you in text messages.

4. Text messages and SMS privacy

Our SMS program is for non-marketing appointment confirmations, scheduling changes, and follow-ups related to your appointment or request. We send these texts only after you opt in. This program does not send cold outreach, promotional offers, or sales solicitations by text.

SMS consent is optional and separate from submitting a form or booking an appointment. You may leave the SMS checkbox unchecked and still submit the form or book. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchase.

Mobile information and consent

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Messaging providers process information on our behalf only as needed to operate the program, route messages, provide support, and maintain consent and opt-out records. We do not sell, rent, or transfer your SMS consent for another business's messaging program.

Message frequency varies with your appointment, request, and replies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to end texts or HELP for assistance. You may also contact sales@vosik.io. See our SMS Terms & Conditions for program details.

5. Service providers and disclosures

We use service providers to operate our website and respond to you. Our current website uses Render for hosting, Cloudflare for network delivery and security, Google Analytics for website analytics, and HighLevel/LeadConnector for scheduling, contact management, and communications. Email and telecommunications providers also process information needed to deliver communications.

We provide these providers with information needed for their services. They are not authorized by us to use SMS consent or mobile information for their own marketing. The SMS restrictions in Section 4 also apply to our other voluntary disclosures.

We may disclose information when required by law, a valid legal request, or to protect legal rights, safety, and the security of our services. Any legally required disclosure is limited to what the law requires. We do not sell or rent personal information collected through this website.

6. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website, including which pages are read and how the site performs. Analytics tools may use cookies and similar technologies and process device information, website activity, and approximate location. Our hosting and security providers may also process technical information to deliver the site and prevent abuse.

You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings and delete cookies already stored on your device. Blocking cookies may affect some embedded scheduling or other features. Your browser settings do not unsubscribe you from text messages; use STOP to manage SMS.

7. How long we keep information

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to answer your request, manage appointments and our business relationship, operate our services, and meet legal or recordkeeping obligations. Retention depends on the type of information, its purpose, and applicable requirements.

We may retain limited consent, opt-out, and suppression records after a deletion request so we can honor your communication preferences, prevent unwanted messages, and demonstrate compliance. Information in backups may remain until those backups are replaced in the normal course of operations.

8. Security and processing locations

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Vosik is based in the United States. We and our service providers may process information in the United States and other locations where they operate. Privacy laws in those locations may differ from those where you live.

9. Your choices and privacy requests

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete information you have provided by emailing sales@vosik.io. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may also have rights to obtain a copy of your information, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or appeal a privacy-request decision.

We may need to verify your identity or an authorized agent's authority before responding. We will respond within the time required by applicable law and explain any applicable exception. If you wish to appeal our response, contact the same email address and identify your request as a privacy appeal.

For texts, reply STOP to the sending number. For email, use any unsubscribe option provided or contact us to change your preferences. Withdrawing SMS consent does not cancel an appointment or change your separate email preferences.

10. Children's privacy

Our website and business services are intended for adults acting in a business capacity and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us so we can review and address it.

11. Other websites and services

Our website may link to or embed third-party services. Those services may have their own privacy notices. This policy describes Vosik's handling of information and does not govern unrelated websites or independently operated services.

12. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our website, communications, practices, or legal requirements. The updated version will appear on this page with a revised date. When required, we will provide additional notice or obtain new consent. A policy update does not expand the scope of your existing SMS consent.
