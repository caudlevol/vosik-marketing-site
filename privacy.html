Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective October 11, 2026 · Vosik AI LLC

This policy covers the vosik.io marketing website. The Vosik application (app.vosik.io) is governed by our customer agreements.

What we collect

This site collects no personal information on its own — it has no forms that store data. If you book a demo or a quote call, the scheduling form is operated by our scheduling provider (LeadConnector) and collects the details you enter: name, email, phone, and company. We use them to hold the meeting and follow up — nothing else.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages get read. It sets cookies and collects standard usage data (pages visited, browser type, approximate location). You can block these cookies in your browser without affecting the site.

Service providers

The site is hosted on Render behind Cloudflare. Scheduling runs on LeadConnector. Analytics run on Google Analytics. Each processes data only to provide its service.

Retention and your rights

Booking details are kept while we work together and deleted on request. To see, correct, or delete anything you have given us, email sales@vosik.io and we will handle it promptly.
